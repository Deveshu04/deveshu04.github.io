Plates 01 – 05

One butterfly, five signatures.

Every tool carries the same mark. The lower-right wing is the only thing that changes: one colour and one small glyph, so you can tell them apart in a dock at a glance.

Plate 01 · Record
#FF6B6B

Butterfly Record

Screen recording that never asks for an account and never uploads a frame.

  • Record a tab, a whole screen or one window, with mic and system audio mixed in
  • Unlimited length: chunks stream to disk at flat memory
  • A frame-exact editor with split, trim, undo and a waveform
  • On-device transcription, filler-word removal, free SRT and VTT
Chrome extensionWindows app in scaffoldSelf-hosted sharing, opt-in
Plate 02 · Speak
#F7A928

Butterfly Speak

Dictation for India. Hold a key, speak in any of 23 languages, release, and it's typed.

  • English and 22 Indian languages, auto-detected or pinned, mixed mid-sentence
  • Push-to-talk into any app: browser, editor, chat, terminal
  • A fully offline mode with speech models measured in megabytes
  • Optional AI polish that removes fillers without changing meaning
WindowsCloud, your own Sarvam key, or on-deviceLives in the tray
Plate 03 · Code
#56D882

Butterfly Code

An agentic coding CLI built for accuracy on average models and a small token bill.

  • Nine tools, not thirty, because accuracy drops past ten
  • A ranked map of your repo on the first turn, about a thousand tokens
  • Autonomous loops that gate every task and commit on green
  • Any provider, including the ones running on your own hardware

Butterfly Meet

A live assistant that sits over your calls and reads the room with you.

  • Real-time help from what is on screen and what is being said
  • Profiles for interviews, sales calls, meetings, presentations and negotiations
  • A transparent, always-on-top overlay you can click straight through
  • Your own Gemini key, or models running locally

Butterfly Sciences

A research workbench where the citations are real and the exports are ready to submit.

  • Six scholarly sources searched together, de-duplicated by DOI
  • A council of models that review each other before a chairman answers
  • Publication-ready LaTeX, Word and Excel, plus Python that runs in the browser
  • API keys live only in your browser, forwarded per request

Privacy

What Butterfly Labs collects, where it is kept, who else handles it and how to have it deleted. It covers this website and Butterfly Speak, our dictation app for Windows.

Who we are

Butterfly Labs is a small software lab in India. You can reach us through GitHub at github.com/Deveshu04.

Butterfly Speak

Speak has three modes, and you choose which one it uses. What leaves your computer depends on that choice.

Cloud mode

To sign you in and to apply the free weekly allowance, Butterfly Labs keeps:

  • what Google provides when you sign in: your e-mail address, your name, the link to your profile picture and your Google account id;
  • a Supabase user id;
  • one number per week: how many words you dictated.

Supabase stores these for us in its Mumbai region.

While you dictate, your audio and text pass through the Butterfly Labs relay, which runs on Cloudflare, to Sarvam AI for transcription and formatting. Butterfly Labs does not store your audio or your text. Sarvam AI processes them under its own terms.

To enforce the allowance, the relay also keeps your Supabase user id and this week's usage (words, formatting requests and seconds of audio) in Cloudflare's storage. When a week ends, its word count stays there until it has been copied to Supabase. The relay erases all of this by itself within about a week after you stop using Cloud mode.

Bring your own key

The app sends your audio from your computer straight to Sarvam AI, under your own Sarvam account and key. It does not pass through Butterfly Labs, and we receive nothing.

On-device

Speech is recognised on your computer, and your dictation is not sent anywhere.

In every mode

  • The app has no analytics and no telemetry.
  • Your dictation history and notes are kept on your computer.
  • Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared for advertising.

Google user data

Speak uses the data it receives from your Google account only to sign you in. Its use of that data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your account

From version 0.1.0 of Speak, Settings → Speech engine → Cloud → Delete my Cloud account deletes your account, your Google profile details and your usage counts, in Supabase and in Cloudflare, at once. Until the week ends (Monday 00:00 UTC), the relay keeps a one-way keyed hash of your e-mail address with that week's usage, so that deleting cannot reset the weekly allowance. Our service providers keep short-lived operational logs; Supabase keeps its authentication logs for about a day.

If you can't sign in any more, write to the support address shown on Google's sign-in screen, or open an issue at github.com/Deveshu04/Butterfly-Speak/issues, and your account is deleted from Supabase within 30 days. The relay's copy erases itself within about a week of your last use, so nothing is left after that. Please don't post your e-mail address in the issue; we'll ask for it privately.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so GitHub and Google see the network address your browser connects from, under their own privacy policies. The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics.
